IMPORTANT:
Your live website was still loading the OLD index.html and OLD script.js.

WHAT TO REPLACE
---------------
Replace these three files in the ROOT of your existing website project:

1. index.html
2. style.css
3. script.js

Do not delete or rename your existing assets folder.
The new website uses your existing project images from:
assets/images/projects/

GITHUB + VERCEL METHOD
----------------------
1. Open the same GitHub repository connected to your Vercel project.
2. Upload index.html, style.css and script.js together.
3. Choose "Commit changes".
4. Open Vercel > your project > Deployments.
5. Wait until the newest deployment says "Ready".
6. Open the website and press Ctrl + Shift + R for a hard refresh.

HOW TO CONFIRM V2 IS LIVE
-------------------------
The first screen must be BLACK and show the large headline:
"DESIGNS THAT MAKE PEOPLE STOP."

If the old white/blue hero still appears, the files were uploaded to the
wrong repository, wrong branch, or Vercel has not deployed the new commit.
